Jesus is Prophet, Priest, and King. Through Baptism, Christians share in this mission. But what does that actually look like in a group chat, a friendship, or a school decision?

✝TruthPrayerService
One mission · Three ways to live it
01 · Witness

Prophet

Jesus reveals God's truth and challenges wrongdoing. We witness to truth, speak with courage, and defend dignity.

Truth spoken in love · CCC 785
02 · Self-gift

Priest

Jesus reconciles us to God by offering Himself once for all. We share in His mission through prayer, worship, and offering our lives in love.

Intercession and self-offering · CCC 784
03 · Service

King

Jesus leads by serving, not dominating. We use responsibility and influence for the common good, especially for those overlooked.

Leadership that serves · CCC 786

How the activity works

01 / 03 · A decision under pressure5 MINUTES

The screenshot
isn't the whole story.

A truth spreads faster than its context. What responsibility do you have when speaking up may make you unpopular?

The situation · Thursday, 8:42 p.m.

A message in your group chat

Your grade's group chat starts circulating a screenshot that appears to show a student, Jordan, changing numbers on a charity fundraising document. You have not seen the original file or the complete exchange.

Alex · 8:42Someone just sent this. Did Jordan fake the fundraiser total?
Riley · 8:43It looks bad. Everybody should know before tomorrow.
You · drafting...I don't have the full story.

A friend asks you to repost the screenshot. The next morning, Jordan will be in your first class. Several students have already turned against them.

What makes this difficult

Truth has a cost.

If you challenge the rumour, people may accuse you of defending dishonesty. If you say nothing, the rumour may harm someone's reputation.

Dignity at stake: Jordan's reputation, the truth about the fundraiser, and the group chat's trust.

1 Your decision

What is your best first move, before you know every fact?

Defend your decision

Why is your choice responsible, even with missing information? Include a likely consequence for another person.

Suggested: 2–3 sentences0 words

Make it concrete

What exact message would you send, or what two actions would you take by the next school day?

Suggested: 1–2 sentences0 words
2 Connect to Jesus

Which of Christ's three roles most directly guides your response?

Bring Scripture into the decision

After choosing and explaining your first move, compare it with the Gospel.

Jesus' example

Jesus proclaims God's truth and good news to the poor (Luke 4:18–19). His prophetic mission goes beyond predicting the future; it reveals truth and calls for conversion.

Our Baptism

Christians share His prophetic office by holding to the faith and becoming His witnesses in the world (CCC 785).

A difficult question

How can you challenge misinformation without spreading it further, shaming others, or pretending to know what you have not verified?

Read: Luke 4:18–19 (NRSV-CE) ↗ · CCC 785 ↗

Now rethink your answer

Use the Scripture lens to explain one thing you would keep, strengthen, or change in your response. Refer to Jesus' mission, not just your opinion.

02 / 03 · A friendship under strain5 MINUTES

You can't carry
it all for them.

Someone trusts you with a problem you cannot fix alone. How do you care without overstepping or abandoning them?

The situation · Tuesday, after school

A message from a close friend

Sam has missed two practices and seems exhausted. They tell you they are helping care for a younger sibling at home, working weekend shifts, and falling behind on assignments.

Sam · 4:16I don't need a huge rescue plan. I'm just overwhelmed and don't want everybody knowing.
Sam · 4:18Could we talk? I really don't know where to start with everything due this week.
You · drafting...I'm here. Let's work out what would actually help.

Sam asks you to keep the conversation private. You want to help, but you also have your own assignments and responsibilities.

What makes this difficult

Care has limits.

Helping could require time and sacrifice. Taking over Sam's responsibilities, however, may not be healthy or honest. Involving another person without permission could harm trust.

Dignity at stake: Sam's agency, privacy, well-being, and your capacity to offer meaningful support.

1 Your decision

How would you respond to Sam this week?

Defend your decision

How does your choice show care and respect? Explain one boundary or risk you would need to manage.

Suggested: 2–3 sentences0 words

Make it concrete

Write two practical actions you would take, including something you would say directly to Sam.

Suggested: 1–2 sentences0 words
2 Connect to Jesus

Which of Christ's three roles most directly guides your response?

Bring Scripture into the decision

Compare the human cost of helping with Christ's model of self-gift.

Jesus' example

Jesus, the true High Priest, offers Himself to reconcile humanity with God (Hebrews 9:11–14). His saving sacrifice is unique and complete.

Our Baptism

Through Christ, baptized Christians offer spiritual sacrifices, pray for others, and dedicate ordinary acts of love to God (CCC 784; Romans 12:1).

A difficult question

How can prayer and self-giving lead to helpful action while respecting Sam's freedom, your limits, and the need for adult support when appropriate?

Read: Hebrews 9:11–14 (NRSV-CE) ↗ · CCC 784 ↗

Now rethink your answer

How does Jesus' priestly self-giving challenge your plan to support Sam? Identify one change, or defend what you would keep.

Suggested: 1–2 sentences0 words

✓Caring for someone does not mean becoming their counsellor or doing their schoolwork. If safety becomes a concern, involve a trusted adult promptly.

03 / 03 · Influence and responsibility5 MINUTES

Who gets
a voice?

You have real influence over a school event. How can leadership serve others without becoming a popularity contest?

The situation · Student leadership meeting

One remaining presentation slot

Your school's student committee is planning a community assembly. Only one five-minute student presentation slot remains.

Proposal A: A highly popular club wants extra time for its successful campaign. Members argue that the extra segment will draw a larger audience.
Proposal B: A small student group wants to present a practical proposal to help new students feel included. It has never had assembly time.

The chair asks you to recommend a decision. You know friends in the popular club expect you to support them, but you are also responsible to the wider school community.

What makes this difficult

Service is a choice.

Popularity and attendance matter, but so do fairness and belonging. You could use your position to protect friendships, reward success, or make room for neglected voices.

Dignity at stake: students whose voices are regularly heard and those rarely included.

1 Your decision

What would you recommend to the committee, and why?

Defend your decision

Whose needs and rights matter here? Explain the trade-off you accept and why it serves the school.

Suggested: 2–3 sentences0 words

Make it concrete

Write two things you would say at the meeting so others understand your decision, including those who disagree.

Suggested: 1–2 sentences0 words
2 Connect to Jesus

Which of Christ's three roles most directly guides your response?

Bring Scripture into the decision

Compare your understanding of leadership with Jesus' understanding.

Jesus' example

Jesus teaches that greatness is expressed through service, not control or status (Mark 10:42–45). He is King, but His kingship is radically humble.

Our Baptism

Christians share in Christ's royal office by serving, exercising self-mastery, and caring especially for the poor and suffering (CCC 786).

A difficult question

When your own friends benefit from your authority, what could you give up to serve the wider community more fairly?

Read: Mark 10:42–45 (NRSV-CE) ↗ · CCC 786 ↗

Now rethink your answer

Would your recommendation still be fair if you were not friends with either group? Explain how Jesus' example changes or strengthens your reasoning.

✓Servant leadership may require making an unpopular decision, explaining it honestly, and listening to people who disagree.

Three ways to serve.

In each case, one role of Jesus may have been more obvious, but all three are connected. Truth without love can wound. Service without truth can mislead. Prayer without action can become an excuse to avoid responsibility.

Prophet

Priest

Join prayer and self-offering to real acts of love without claiming to replace Christ's sacrifice.

05 / Debrief · Discuss together5 MINUTES · CLASS CONVERSATION

What would
Jesus ask of us?

You have made three decisions. Now put the ideas in conversation with your classmates. Listen for reasons, not just agreement.

01 / Truth

When should you speak up?

In the rumour case, could confronting the group make the harm worse? What does truthful, courageous love require when the facts are uncertain?

02 / Self-gift

When does helping go too far?

In the friendship case, what is the difference between generous service and taking over? How can prayer lead to responsible action?

03 / Leadership

Whose interests come first?

In the committee case, would you make the same choice if your friends had nothing to gain? How do you recognize the common good?
